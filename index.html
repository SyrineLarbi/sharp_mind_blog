Sharp Mind · judgement, presence, conversation and how the world works

Think fast. Ask better. Be believed. Sixteen handbooks, one path.

This library exists to make you a specific kind of person: someone who answers quickly without answering badly, who asks the one question nobody else thought of, who is believed when they speak — and who always has something worth saying, because they understand how the actual world works. None of that is personality. All of it is trainable technique, and every technique here is written as a move you can make on Monday morning.

Read this part first

Most advice on being sharper is really advice on being a different person — be more confident, be more curious, be more present. That is unusable. This library takes the opposite position: the people who seem quick are not processing faster than you. They have pre-loaded more, they know a small number of question shapes that unlock almost any situation, they put their answer first so their thinking sounds organised, and they have practised the four or five sentences that get them out of trouble.

Every one of those is a mechanism, and a mechanism can be learned. If you want the psychology underneath them, that lives in master-yourself/, and this library points at it constantly rather than repeating it.
